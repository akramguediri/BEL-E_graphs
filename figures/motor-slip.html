Synchronous vs asynchronous · slip

The stator makes a rotating field that spins at the fixed synchronous speed ns. A synchronous motor's rotor is magnetised and locks to it exactly. A squirrel-cage (asynchronous) rotor has no magnet — it can only make torque by lagging behind the field so the field cuts its bars and induces current. That lag is the slip. Add load and watch it fall further behind.
