1 · The big idea

A function is just a machine

Put a number x in. The machine follows one rule. A number y comes out. That's the whole concept — "f(x)" just means "the machine f, fed x."

2 → double it, +1 → 5
feed in x = 2.0  →  rule f(x)=2x+1  →  out comes y = 5.0. The dot is that answer, drawn as a point (x, y).

Slide it — the dot traces every answer, and the answers form a line.

2 · Straight lines

f(x) = m·x + b

Every straight line is two numbers. m is the slope — how steep (how much y climbs when x goes right by 1). b is where it crosses the vertical axis.

walk 1 step right, the line climbs m = 1.0. It crosses the y-axis at b = 0.0.  

Make m negative → it goes downhill. Make m = 0 → flat. That's all a slope is.

3 · Parabolas (the U-shape)

f(x) = a·x² + b·x + c

The x² bends the line into a U. a sets how tight the U is and which way it opens (up or down). The roots (orange dots) are where it touches zero — the famous formula just finds those.

Discriminant b²−4ac decides everything: positive → 2 roots, zero → 1, negative → the U floats clear of zero (no real roots). Watch the dots vanish when it goes negative.

4 · Exponential & its mirror, the log

bx grows fast — log undoes it

bˣ (blue) is repeated multiplying: growth that explodes. logb(x) (green) is the undo button — it answers "what power gives me this number?" They're mirror images across the dashed line y = x.

with base b = 2.0:   b3 = 8.0 (multiply b three times), and the log undoes it: logb(8.0) = 3.

That mirror is why "take the log of both sides" solves exponential equations — it's literally the undo.
