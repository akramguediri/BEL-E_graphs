Pallets in a container · floor utilisation

A 20-ft container floor is about 5.90 m × 2.35 m. How well pallets fill it depends entirely on which pallet and which way you turn them. The EUR pallet (1.20 × 0.80 m) and the industrial / block pallet (1.20 × 1.00 m) pack very differently — that's why real utilisation lands around 76–95%. Try the layouts.
