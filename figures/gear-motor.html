G5 Technology I · § Hydraulic motors

Gear motor · pressure in, rotation out

The vane motor is one rotary type; here's another. Two meshing gears sit in a tight housing. High-pressure oil enters on one side and can't get through the mesh, so it pushes the teeth round and is carried in the tooth pockets along the outer walls to the low-pressure outlet. Fluid power becomes torque. Turn it.
